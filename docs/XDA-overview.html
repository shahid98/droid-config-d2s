Sailfish OS 5.1.0.11 for the Galaxy Note 10+

An unofficial port of Sailfish OS “Pispala” to the Exynos Note 10+, built on a hybris-18.1 (LineageOS 18.1) base. It works as a phone — calls, SMS, LTE, WiFi, camera, GPS — with real gaps listed below. Installing erases the device, and there is no Android app support.

Before anything else

Not for SM-N975U or N975W (Snapdragon), and not for the N976 5G variants. Those have different hardware and this build will not run on them.

The base ROM matters

This port is built against the vendor files from the unofficial LineageOS 18.1 build for d2s by ivanmeler. A different LineageOS build or stock firmware can bring back a black camera viewfinder, because the camera driver and the vendor camera library have to match.

There is a root shell over USB

Like every hybris port, the phone runs a telnet shell on the USB network (192.168.2.15, port 2323) with no password. Anyone who can plug a PC into your phone has root on it. Decide whether that suits you before daily-driving this.

What works

Calls, SMS and mobile data

Working

Dialling and SMS work with two-way audio through the earpiece, and emergency calling reaches a dispatcher. LTE data works and survives a reboot. The call audio path is set up by a service written for this port — Sailfish’s usual route needs a 64-bit vendor HAL this phone does not have.

WiFi and Bluetooth

Working

WiFi connects with the correct MAC address. Bluetooth powers on, finds and pairs devices, and plays audio to a paired headset.

Display, touch and sensors

Working

Full 1440 × 3040 at the right scaling, auto-rotation, double-tap to wake, and the proximity sensor (the screen blanks against your ear). The status bar knows where the punch-hole camera is, so the clock is not hidden behind it.

Camera — all three rear lenses

Working

Stills and video with sound, front and back. Samsung’s camera service hides the telephoto from ordinary apps; this port asks for it by name, so the camera app can switch between them. The front camera uses the full 10 MP sensor rather than the cropped mode Android offers first, and 4K recording runs at about 19 Mbit/s.

Video playback and audio

Working

Hardware decoding: 1080p and 1440p are smooth in the browser, and VP9 plays in third-party YouTube clients. Speaker playback, microphone recording and vibration all work.

GPS

Working

Including assistance data, so fixes are not a cold start every time. Indoors a cold start took about two minutes and landed within 16 m. Location is off by default — switch it on in Settings.

USB: MTP, developer mode, charging only

Working

File transfer measured around 60 MB/s writing and 34 MB/s reading. Developer mode gives you a USB network and SSH.

Battery and charging

Working

Level and charging state report correctly, and the phone idles properly — an earlier build kept one CPU core busy from boot.

What does not work

Android apps

Waydroid only

Jolla’s Alien Dalvik is licensed only to officially supported devices. Waydroid works — Android 13 in a container, with touch, the browser and app installs — but it is not in the image: run devel-su /usr/bin/d2s-waydroid-setup.sh, which pulls about 1 GB of images. Inside Waydroid, the camera and shared storage (Gallery, Documents) do not work.

Audio routing

Partly automatic

Wired headphones and the earpiece for media are not switched automatically — playback stays on the speakers. Bluetooth audio does work: pair a headset and playback moves to it, and back to the speaker when it disconnects.

In-call volume slider

Inert

The slider does not change the volume, and the in-call speaker button is not wired to the routing this port uses.

Fingerprint reader

Unsupported

Sailfish has no driver for this ultrasonic sensor.

NFC and the S-Pen

Untested

Assume they do not work.

Smaller gaps

Known

IPv6 on mobile data has no route (everything falls back to IPv4, which is fine in practice). USB tethering is not included. 4K60 in the browser stutters slightly. The boot splash is a still logo, not an animation. In the Bluetooth device list, nearby phones and trackers using randomised addresses show as bare MAC addresses — they broadcast no name, and Sailfish lists them where Android hides them.

Flashing

You need a PC with adb and Heimdall (Linux) or Odin (Windows), and an unlocked bootloader — enable OEM unlocking in Android’s developer options first. Everything on the phone is erased, including internal storage.

  1. Flash the stock firmware the port was built against

    N975FXXS9HWG9 (Android 12), with Heimdall or Odin. This gives you a known-good modem and bootloader.

  2. Flash LineageOS 18.1 and its recovery

    ivanmeler’s unofficial build for d2s. Boot it once to confirm the phone is healthy, then go back to recovery.

  3. Format data in recovery

    The option that makes you type “yes” — not just a wipe.

  4. Install Sailfish by hand

    adb sideload is unreliable on this device: it stalls around 47% and writes nothing. Push the parts instead — recovery has no bzip2, so unpack the rootfs on the PC first.

    # on the PC
unzip sailfishos-d2s-release-5.1.0.11.zip -d sfos
bunzip2 sfos/sailfishos-d2s-release-5.1.0.11.tar.bz2

# with the phone in LineageOS recovery
adb push sfos/sailfishos-d2s-release-5.1.0.11.tar /data/sfos.tar
adb push sfos/hybris-boot.img /data/hybris-boot.img

adb shell
  mkdir -p /data/.stowaways/sailfishos
  tar xvf /data/sfos.tar -C /data/.stowaways/sailfishos/
  dd if=/data/hybris-boot.img of=/dev/block/by-name/boot
  sync
  rm /data/sfos.tar /data/hybris-boot.img
  exit

  5. Reboot

    The Sailfish logo appears a few seconds in; the first boot then takes a few minutes to prepare the device before the welcome wizard. Later boots reach the homescreen in about half a minute.

After installing

  • Mobile data not working? Check the APN in Settings → Mobile network → your SIM → Access Point Name. Sailfish picks one from a public database and it is not always right — on Bell in Canada it chooses the tablet APN inet.bell.ca where phone plans need pda.bell.ca. Reboot after changing it: the APN only reaches the modem when telephony starts.
  • GPS: switch Location on in Settings → Location. Nothing gets a fix until you do.
  • USB mode: Settings → USB switches between MTP, developer mode and charging only.
  • Storage: the crash reporter is included in this build. If you never intend to send logs anywhere, clear its reports occasionally — they can grow to hundreds of MB.

Reporting problems

Say what you did and what happened, and include the exact model (SM-N975F), which LineageOS build you flashed first, and what the screen showed — the Sailfish logo, the welcome wizard, or nothing. For anything radio-related, name your carrier and whether you were on WiFi, 4G or 3G at the time.
